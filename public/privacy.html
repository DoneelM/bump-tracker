Bump Tracker privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026.

Bump Tracker is a free pregnancy tracker for Aotearoa New Zealand. It works in your browser. It has no accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers. We don’t run a server that receives your data, so we never see it.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (due date, weight, scans, contractions, baby movements, journal, questions for your midwife, blood pressure and glucose readings, supplements taken, checklists, baby name shortlist, care team phone numbers, birth details and bump photos) is stored in this browser on your device. Clearing your browser’s data for this site deletes it. You can download a backup file, which goes wherever you save it.

Optional sync with your Google Drive

Sync is off unless you turn it on. If you do:

  • You sign in with Google and allow access to drive.appdata only. That is a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only Bump Tracker can use. The app can’t see your other Drive files.
  • Your details, logs, journal, checklists and care team numbers are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded. Photos are not synced. Two files are stored in that hidden folder: the encrypted data, and a key that can only be unlocked with the recovery code you were shown. Without the code, the data can’t be read by Google, by us or by anyone else.
  • The Google sign-in token is kept in your browser’s memory for the session and is never stored or sent to us. We don’t receive your name, email address or any other Google account information.
  • Google can see that you use Bump Tracker, and when you sync, under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Bump Tracker’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to sync your own Bump Tracker data between your devices.

Turning sync off and deleting

  • “Turn off sync on this device” (Sync page) stops syncing here. Your data stays on the device and in your Drive.
  • “Delete synced copy” (Sync page) deletes both files from your Google Drive.
  • You can also remove the app’s access, and its hidden data, in your Google Account: Google Drive settings → Manage apps → Bump Tracker → Delete hidden app data and Disconnect from Drive.

Health information

Bump Tracker gives general information only, not medical advice. Contact your midwife/LMC with any concerns, or call 111 in an emergency.
