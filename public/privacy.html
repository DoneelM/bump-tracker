Hey Baby privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026.

Hey Baby is a free pregnancy tracker for Aotearoa New Zealand. It works in your browser. It has no accounts with us, no advertising, no third-party trackers and no tracking of who you are. We don’t run a server that receives your data, so we never see it. The only thing the app sends us is an anonymous daily count, described below, which you can turn off.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (due date, weight, scans, contractions, baby movements, journal, questions for your midwife, blood pressure and glucose readings, supplements taken, checklists, baby name shortlist, care team phone numbers, birth details, the baby log, baby’s firsts, immunisations done, your weekly check-ins and bump photos) is stored in this browser on your device. Clearing your browser’s data for this site deletes it. You can download a backup file, which goes wherever you save it.

Anonymous daily count

To know how many people use Hey Baby, the app sends one message to our own server the first time you open it each day, and one each month. It contains only the app version, whether the app is in pregnancy or baby mode, and whether it’s the daily or monthly count. It has no name, no account, no device ID and nothing you’ve entered. We don’t record your IP address with it, and the counts are only ever looked at as totals. Cloudflare, which hosts the app, handles the message like any page load. Turn it off in Settings → Anonymous count; the choice is saved on that device.

Optional sync with your Google Drive

Sync is off unless you turn it on. If you do:

  • You sign in with Google and allow access to drive.appdata only. That is a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only Bump Tracker can use. The app can’t see your other Drive files.
  • Your details, logs, journal, checklists and care team numbers are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded. Photos are not synced. Two files are stored in that hidden folder: the encrypted data, and a key that can only be unlocked with the recovery code you were shown. Without the code, the data can’t be read by Google, by us or by anyone else.
  • The Google sign-in token is kept in your browser’s memory for the session and is never stored or sent to us. We don’t receive your name, email address or any other Google account information.
  • Google can see that you use Hey Baby, and when you sync, under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Hey Baby’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to sync your own Hey Baby data between your devices.

Turning sync off and deleting

  • “Turn off sync on this device” (Sync page) stops syncing here. Your data stays on the device and in your Drive.
  • “Delete synced copy” (Sync page) deletes both files from your Google Drive.
  • You can also remove the app’s access, and its hidden data, in your Google Account: Google Drive settings → Manage apps → Hey Baby → Delete hidden app data and Disconnect from Drive.

Health information

Hey Baby gives general information only, not medical advice. Contact your midwife/LMC with any concerns, or call 111 in an emergency.

Feedback

If you use “Contact us”, Hey Baby opens our feedback form on Google Forms with your answers filled in. Nothing is sent unless you press Submit there. The form includes what it’s about, your message, your email address only if you add it, and, if you leave it ticked, basic app info (version, light or dark, screen size, whether it’s installed). Never your due date, logs or anything you’ve entered. The form doesn’t collect your email address or Google account. Google receives what you submit and processes it under its own privacy policy. If you choose to email us instead, the email comes from your own address. We use feedback only to reply and improve the app, and you can ask us to delete it.
